Standard Operating Procedure for incident resolution: 1. Log the incident with severity level (LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH/CRITICAL). 2. Notify the assigned driver within 5 minutes. 3. If shipment is delayed over 2 hours, auto-escalate to operations manager. 4. For damaged parcels, photograph evidence and initiate refund workflow. 5. Resolution target: HIGH severity within 1 hour, MEDIUM within 4 hours, LOW within 24 hours.
